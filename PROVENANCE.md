# Research process and limitations

This project grew out of an investigation I directed using AI tools. I set the
research direction, asked for challenges and alternative approaches, and requested
progressively stronger checks. AI systems contributed substantially to proposing
ideas, developing mathematical arguments, writing and revising code, checking
calculations, searching the literature and preparing documentation. I did not
independently derive or manually verify every mathematical step, line of code or
claim. I take responsibility for publishing and maintaining the material under
my name and for addressing corrections brought to my attention.

The repository distinguishes the evidence for each result. Specified algebraic
theorems and the order-sharpness construction have been checked in Lean; selected
proof exports have also been accepted by additional checker implementations.
Other arguments and programs have written derivations, exact calculations or
tests. These checks were organized within the same development process, and
some implementations share dependencies. They are not independent external human
review. Formal verification supports the propositions actually encoded, subject
to their stated assumptions and trusted components; it does not automatically
verify every statement in this repository.

The mathematical results concern bounded count laws and the observation models
specified here. The sharpness family is established prior art and is credited
accordingly. Originality of the general identification result remains unresolved.
No physical system, detector calibration or practical measurement protocol has
been validated. All generated examples and simulations are illustrative or
synthetic, not experimental measurements.

Errors, omissions and limitations may remain. This is a research artifact offered
for inspection and reproduction, not a claim of peer review, universal correctness
or suitability for a particular application. The materials are provided as-is
under the applicable licenses. Corrections, counterexamples and independent
mathematical or technical scrutiny are welcome.

This statement describes the development process. Authorship and maintenance
remain with [The Quantum Falcon](https://github.com/thequantumfalcon); AI systems
are not listed as authors or co-authors.
